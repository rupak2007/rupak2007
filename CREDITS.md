# Credits

The scene, the cat and the icons are human-made pixel art released under CC0; the window chrome, 5×7 lettering, dividers and dialog box are drawn in code by `scripts/build.py`. The one exception is the character (the info-card portrait and the figure on the divider): Rupak generated it with ChatGPT, and `scripts/character.py` resampled it to its pixel grid and toned it to the profile's palette.
